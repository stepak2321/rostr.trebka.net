## Rostr 1.2.6

### New
- **Configurable instant replay.** Choose to keep the last 15, 30, 60 or 120 seconds in Settings > Gallery. The default is 30 seconds. The selected duration is used when saving replay clips and is remembered in your settings.
- **Replay buffer status.** The Gallery shows whether the replay buffer is active, waiting for Roblox, starting, or paused while you make a normal recording.

### Improved
- **Roblox-only recording.** Recordings and instant replay capture the Roblox Player window and its process audio, without audio from unrelated apps.
- **Game audio enabled by default.** New settings enable Roblox game audio. Existing profiles are migrated to enable it once; after migration, your chosen audio setting is preserved.
- **Replay controls and descriptions.** The Gallery hotkey hint, replay status messages and settings description reflect the selected replay duration.

### Notes
- Recording and process-audio capture require Windows 10 version 2004 (build 19041) or later, or Windows 11.
- Instant replay pauses while you make a normal recording and resumes when that recording ends.
