Rostr is a launcher for Roblox on Windows. Browse servers by ping, FPS and region, jump into a random one with a single click, and keep an eye on your game with a small in-game overlay.

Everything that happens between Play and the game.

All the controls the Roblox website leaves out, together in one window.

Know the server before you join

Every server lists its players, ping, FPS and region up front, so you never join blind. Rostr detects regions on its own while the list loads, with nothing to press. Sort by most or fewest players or best ping, then narrow it down by region or maximum ping. Green means smooth, amber means fine, red means trouble.

Your frame rate, your rules

Set your FPS cap with the slider (30 to 360) or type in an even higher number. Switch on FPS unlock, low graphics, max performance, high quality or reduced telemetry with a single click. Power users can edit the raw FastFlags directly.

Switch accounts in a click

Add as many accounts as you like and swap between them from the sidebar, with no logging in again. Saved logins are encrypted with your Windows login, and Multi Roblox lets you run more than one client at once.

Your games, one click away

Your recent games and favorites sit together with the time you’ve spent in each. Play drops you into a random server, Servers lets you pick your own, and Rejoin takes you back to the last one. You can also paste a private server link or see who’s online.

Stays out of your way

Rostr installs the Roblox client for you and clears out old versions, so they never pile up on your disk.

Make it look like yours

Eight themes (Dark, Midnight, AMOLED, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Rose and Light) repaint the app instantly, and nine languages follow your Windows language on first start. Settings are sorted into tabs on the left, so every option is easy to find.

Updates itself and shows what changed

Rostr checks GitHub for a new version and asks before installing it. Press See change log to read what is new first. The README and the change log open right inside the app, so you never need a browser.

An overlay you control.

A small click-through box sits in the corner of the Roblox window you pick, and only shows while Roblox is in front. Choose which lines it shows, pick the corner it sits in, and bind the hide and show key to whatever suits you. The default is Ctrl Shift O.

About Rostr

Rostr is an independent launcher made for people who care about which server they land in. It handles the client, the login and the settings, then gets out of the way while you play.

Ping marked with a tilde is an estimate: Roblox servers do not answer ping, so Rostr measures the last router on the way instead.
