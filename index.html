Everything between Play and the game.

All the controls the Roblox website leaves out, together in one window.

NEW

Trending

Player counts of popular games, counted every hour. Open a game for a 24 hour, 7 day or 30 day graph.

Server browser

Sort by players or ping, filter by region, and copy a link so a friend lands in the same server. Regions are detected for you.

Server hop and Rejoin

Join a random server you have not been in yet, then stay or hop again. Rejoin takes you back to your last one.

Dim menu

Press Left Ctrl + Tab in game for a darker, blurred screen with your clock, friends who are playing, the game wiki, notes and quick actions.

Stats overlay

A small click-through box with ping, CPU, RAM, session time and region. You choose the lines and the corner.

Gallery and instant replay

Screenshots, recordings with game sound, and a replay buffer that saves the last 15 to 120 seconds with one hotkey.

FPS cap and FastFlags

An FPS slider from 30 to 360, one-click presets, and raw FastFlags. Rostr warns you when Roblox will ignore a flag.

Accounts and Multi Roblox

Switch accounts from the sidebar. Logins are encrypted with Windows, and every client is tagged so old ones never pile up.

Updates itself

Rostr checks GitHub, shows what changed and installs the new version after you confirm. The download is checked with SHA-256.
