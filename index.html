Everything between “Play” and the game.

Everything the Roblox website leaves out, in one window.

Know what you’re joining

Every server shows its players, ping, FPS and region before you click. Rostr works out the regions by itself while the list loads, so there is nothing to press. Sort by most or fewest players, newest or best ping, then filter by region or maximum ping. Green is smooth, amber is fine, red is trouble.

Your frame rate, your rules

Set an FPS cap with the slider (30 to 360) or type a higher number. One click turns on FPS unlock, low graphics, max performance, high quality or less telemetry. Power users can edit the raw FastFlags too.

Switch accounts without logging in again

Add as many accounts as you like and swap between them from the sidebar. Saved logins are encrypted with your Windows login. Turn on Multi Roblox to run more than one client at once.

Your games, one click away

Recent games and favorites, with the time you’ve spent in each. Play drops you into a random server, Servers lets you choose, and Rejoin takes you back to your last one. You can also paste a private server link or check who’s online.

Stays out of your way

Rostr installs the Roblox client for you and clears out old versions, so your disk doesn’t fill up with them.

An overlay you control.

A small click-through box sits in the corner of the Roblox window you pick, and only shows while Roblox is in front. Choose which lines it shows and bind the hide and show key to whatever suits you. The default is Ctrl Shift O.

About Rostr

Rostr is an independent launcher made for people who care about which server they land in. It handles the client, the login and the settings, then gets out of the way while you play.

Ping marked with a tilde is an estimate: Roblox servers do not answer ping, so Rostr measures the last router on the way instead. Roblox does not publish when a server was created, so the age column shows how long Rostr has known a server rather than its true age.
